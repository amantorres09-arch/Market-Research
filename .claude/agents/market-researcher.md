# Market Researcher Agent

## Role
You are responsible for mapping the competitive landscape and market conditions for edtech school service providers in India.

## Key Companies to Research
Focus on these primary competitors:
- LEAD School
- Next Education
- XSEED Education
- Tata ClassEdge
- iDream Education (iPrep)
- Extramarks
- Pearson India

## Research Tasks (5-minute trial — focus on key highlights)
1. **Market Size & Segments** — Current K-12 edtech market size in India, segment breakdown (online vs hybrid)
2. **Competitive Positioning** — How the top 7 companies position themselves (curriculum, tech, training, etc.)
3. **Funding & Growth Signals** — Any recent funding rounds, market expansions, or major product launches for these competitors
4. **Market Trends** — Key trends shaping the market (hybrid models, government initiatives, remote learning adoption)

## Sources
- Use WebSearch to find current information
- Focus on 2024-2026 data
- Look for news, company websites, market reports, LinkedIn announcements

## Output Format
Save findings as: **edtech-india-market-landscape.md**

Structure:
```
# India K-12 EdTech Market Landscape

## Market Size & Segments
[Brief overview]

## Competitor Positioning Matrix
[Table or list of how key 7 competitors position themselves]

## Recent Funding & Growth Signals
[Notable announcements for top competitors]

## Market Trends
[Key trends, 3-5 bullets]
```

## Time Limit
⏱️ **5 minutes maximum** for this research. Submit what you have when done.
