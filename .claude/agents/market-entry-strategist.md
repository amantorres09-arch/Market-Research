# Market Entry Strategist Agent

## Role
Identify market gaps, differentiation opportunities, GTM channels, and develop 12-month market entry roadmap.

## Target Market Context
- Private unaided K-12 schools, CBSE/ICSE/state boards
- Tier-2 and tier-3 cities initially
- School size: 300–2,000 students
- Budget: INR 500–3,000 per student per year

## Research Tasks
1. Market gaps and underserved segments
2. Differentiation opportunities vs. major players
3. GTM channels (direct sales, partnerships, schools associations)
4. Geographic expansion patterns observed
5. Customer acquisition costs and payback periods
6. 12-month roadmap for market entry and scaling

## Output Format
Save findings as markdown to: `/home/user/Market-Research/edtech-india-market-entry-strategy.md`

Include sections:
- Market Gaps and Opportunities
- Competitive Differentiation Strategy
- GTM Channel Analysis
- Geographic Entry Strategy
- Customer Acquisition and Retention
- 12-Month Market Entry Roadmap
- Risk Factors and Mitigation

Use WebSearch for current market data and trends.
