## Key Strategic Insights

### Market Opportunity
- TAM: $900M–$1.5B for Tier-2/3 segment alone
- Market consolidation around sustainable, hybrid-learning models
- Transition from funding-led to profitability-driven growth
- Annual growth rate: 14.9% (2014–2025)

### Competitive Landscape
- **Leaders:** LEAD School (unicorn), Next Education (18K+ schools), Extramarks
- **Gaps:** No competitor owns transparency, teacher enablement, or state-board-first positioning
- **Opportunity:** Reputation-first positioning (vs. sales-first competitors)

### Our Differentiation
1. **Affordability + Quality:** ₹30–₹180/student/month (vs. ₹2K+/month competitors)
2. **Teacher-Centric:** Focus on teacher enablement/certification (vs. student engagement)
3. **State Boards First:** Hindi-medium, state curriculum alignment (vs. CBSE-only focus)
4. **Transparency:** 3rd-party verified case studies, anti-predatory policy (vs. aggressive sales)
5. **Modular Pricing:** Schools buy what they need (vs. bundled lock-in)

### 12-Month GTM Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (M1–4):** Maharashtra + MP pilot (10–15 schools, ₹5–10L investment)
- **Phase 2 (M5–9):** Expand to Gujarat, UP, Rajasthan (50–80 schools, ₹30–50L)
- **Phase 3 (M10–12):** Scale to 15 cities (200–300 schools, ₹1–1.5 Cr run rate)
