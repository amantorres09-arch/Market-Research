## Who We Are

We are a **hybrid services + tech platform** targeting **K-12 private schools in India**.

We provide both:
- On-ground services: curriculum programs, teacher training, academic improvement
- A technology layer: app, LMS, analytics dashboard for school management

We are a **new entrant** building our market position.

---

## What to Research

We are doing competitive analysis of **school service providers** — companies that provide services TO schools. This is NOT about school management software (ERP/SMS vendors like Fedena or Campuscare).

Target company types:
- Curriculum and content providers
- Teacher training and academic transformation companies
- Hybrid tech + services platforms (like us)
- Franchise school networks
- Smart classroom / digital learning providers

## Our Target Schools

- **Type:** Private unaided K-12 schools
- **Boards:** CBSE, ICSE, state boards
- **Geography:** Initially tier-2 and tier-3 cities; metro cities secondary
- **Size:** 300–2,000 students
- **Budget range:** INR 500–3,000 per student per year (all-in)

## Research Agent Team

Spawn these 4 agents in parallel, then synthesizer last:

1. **market-researcher** — competitive landscape, market size, funding, positioning
2. **services-analyst** — services offered, pricing models, packaging, feature comparison
3. **reviews-strategist** — competitor reputation, Google reviews strategy, testimonials playbook
4. **market-entry-strategist** — market gaps, differentiation, GTM channels, 12-month roadmap

After all 4 complete, spawn:

5. **report-synthesizer** — reads all 4 outputs, produces final HTML report
