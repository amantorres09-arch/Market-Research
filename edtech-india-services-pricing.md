# EdTech India — K-12 School Services Pricing Analysis

**Trial Run | 5-Company Analysis | May 2026**

## Pricing Models Overview

The Indian K-12 school services market uses two pricing philosophies:

- **Per-student subscription** (LEAD, iDream): Price per enrolled student per year
- **Per-school flat fee** (Next Education, Tata ClassEdge, XSEED): Annual contract covers entire school

## Key Pricing Insights

**1. LEAD's price gap is significant.**
Headline: Rs 1,400–2,300/student. Realised ASP (FY24 revenue ÷ students): ~Rs 700/student. 60-70% gap suggests discounting or book revenue.

**2. XSEED's workbook cost is a major complaint vector.**
Parents report Rs 4,000+ per child for primary workbooks — more than some schools' entire external services budget. This is the loudest public pricing complaint across all five vendors.

**3. Tata ClassEdge has severe lock-in risk.**
Hardware-software dependency. Documented case: school paid Rs 21 lakh, promised ownership, company reclaimed hardware at contract end.

**4. iDream is the only vendor publishing transparent pricing.**
Rs 8,000–10,000/classroom/year for content. Lifetime license option is market-unique.

**5. Zero public pricing is structural.**
All five use school-by-school negotiation. Favors vendors, disadvantages schools — especially small schools without procurement experience.
